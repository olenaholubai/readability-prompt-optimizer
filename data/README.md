# Data

Study data for *A Replicable Generative Artificial Intelligence Workflow for Kidney Disease Patient Education: Development and Evaluation in Lupus Nephritis*: the 28 selected materials from each of the 2 iterations, with their evaluations.

> **Note.** The materials in this folder are unedited outputs generated by a large language model and are provided to illustrate the study workflow and the development of patient education materials across iterations.

All files are UTF-8 CSV. `question_no` (1–28, matching Supplemental Table 1) and `iteration` (1 = first, 2 = second/final) identify a material and join the files.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `materials.csv` | Question wording and material text (the text that was scored and rated). `pipeline_run_id` links to the run that produced it. |
| `readability_scores.csv` | Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level, SMOG Grade, Gunning Fog Index and Flesch Reading Ease, as reported in the paper. |
| `pemat_item_ratings.csv` | PEMAT-P item-level ratings, 1 row per material × rater × item. `rating`: 1 = agree, 0 = disagree, NA = not applicable. |
| `physician_accuracy.csv` | Accuracy rating per physician: 1 = substantially accurate and complete, 0 = partially accurate or substantively incomplete, −1 = completely inaccurate. |

Notes:

- **PEMAT-P.** Items use the PEMAT-P numbering. Items 13–14 apply only to audiovisual materials, and the visual-aid items (15–19, 25–26) were excluded as described in the paper; neither is included. Material scores in the paper are the percentage of applicable ratings equal to 1, pooled across the 3 raters.
- **Raters.** `Rater 1`–`Rater 3` are the 3 PEMAT-P raters described in the paper. The codes are arbitrary and do not follow author order. `Physician 1` and `Physician 2` are as defined in the paper; Physician 2 was blinded to iteration.
